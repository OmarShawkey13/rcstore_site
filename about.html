About RCKeyBoard

Learn how RCKeyBoard brings smart typing, customizable themes, and 27+ languages to Android.

🏆 Premium Android IME

Reinventing Mobile Typing with Privacy & Elegance

RCKeyBoard was built with a clear mission: to provide Android users with a hyper-responsive, privacy-first typing experience that looks stunning and feels effortless. Combining machine learning predictions with deep aesthetic customization, RCKeyBoard empowers power users to type faster while maintaining complete data ownership.

⚡

Lightning-Fast Performance

Designed for responsive typing across supported Android devices, with personal dictionary learning kept on your device.

🎨

Unlimited Customization

Choose from over 50 curated themes (Amoled, Avocado, Pink Check, Minecraft, Cute Cat) or build your own custom background with custom key fonts and height sizing.

🔒

Privacy By Design

Routine typing and personal dictionary learning stay on your device. Optional network features and technical diagnostics are explained in our privacy policy.

🌍

Global Expressiveness

Supports 27+ languages, including Arabic, English, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Portuguese, Hindi, Turkish, and more, with optional live translation.

Modern Engineering Architecture

RCKeyBoard leverages Android's Input Method Editor (IME) architecture powered by modern Jetpack libraries, Room DB for encrypted local dictionary storage, and Coil for instant image rendering.
