Privacy Policy

Your data, your control

Your privacy is important to us. RCKeyBoard does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data without your explicit consent. All learning and prediction processes are performed locally on your device to ensure maximum security. We do not sell data to third parties.

Data Collection

RCKeyBoard does not collect personal typing data. All predictions and learning happen locally on your device.

Data Storage

No keystroke data is stored on external servers. Your typing patterns remain private.

Third Parties

We do not share any user data with third parties. Your privacy is our priority.

Your Rights

You can clear all local learning data at any time through the app settings.
