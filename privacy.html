Privacy Policy

Your privacy is guaranteed. Read our complete offline local processing policy.

🛡️ Privacy Commitment

Your Data Remains Yours. Period.

Effective Date: July 27, 2026 • Version 2.4

At RCKeyBoard, we believe your keystrokes are deeply personal. Whether you're typing private messages, passwords, or financial information, our application is engineered so that your data never leaves your device.

01

Zero Keystroke Logging

RCKeyBoard does NOT collect, record, or transmit typed text, passwords, credit card numbers, address book contacts, or search queries to any remote servers or third parties. All text predictions and autocorrect operations execute entirely in your device's local memory.

02

Local Machine Learning & Dictionaries

When RCKeyBoard learns your typing habits and vocabulary, the resulting personal dictionary is saved exclusively in encrypted local app storage on your device. You can clear or reset your learned dictionary data at any time directly through the RCKeyBoard settings menu.

03

Permissions & Network Access

RCKeyBoard requires only standard IME (Input Method Editor) permissions to operate as your system keyboard. Network permissions are used strictly for optional features (such as live text translation or downloading new theme packages from Google Play) and never for uploading typing data.

04

Third-Party Analytics & Crash Diagnostics

To maintain stability and fix bugs, RCKeyBoard uses anonymized, aggregated crash reporting through Google Play Console. This data contains zero personal text information and includes only technical diagnostic data (e.g. device model, Android OS version, app version).

05

Your Rights & Data Control

You maintain 100% control over your data. You can clear all local app storage, uninstall the app, or revoke optional permissions at any time. For questions regarding our privacy practices, contact us at privacy@rcmods.online.
